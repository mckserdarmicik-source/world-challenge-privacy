WORLD CHALLENGE: Account Deletion

This page explains how to permanently delete your WORLD CHALLENGE account and the personal data associated with it, as described in our Privacy Policy.

Important: Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. Once your account is deleted, you can no longer sign in to it, and your login credentials and profile details are removed.

Method 1: Delete Inside the App

  1. Open the WORLD CHALLENGE app and go to the Profile tab.
  2. In the Account management section, tap "Delete Account".
  3. Read the confirmation message, then tap "Request Deletion" to confirm.
  4. The app sends the request to our server. When the server confirms, your account is deleted permanently and you are signed out on that device.

Method 2: Request by Email

If you cannot access the app (for example, after uninstalling it), send an email to mck.serdar.micik@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request", using the email address associated with your WORLD CHALLENGE account. We may ask you to verify account ownership before processing the request.

What Data Is Deleted

  • Authentication records: your email address, password credentials, and Google or Facebook sign-in links.
  • Profile information: your username and selected country.
  • Game information: your points, spin credits, streaks, and related game progress.
  • Game data stored locally on your device.

Data Retention

We will remove or de-identify associated personal information where technically possible and legally required. Certain records may need to be retained where required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or legitimate operational purposes. See the Account Deletion and Data Retention sections of our Privacy Policy for details.

Contact

For questions about account deletion or your personal data, email mck.serdar.micik@gmail.com.

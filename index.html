WORLD CHALLENGE – Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 10, 2026

1. Age Requirement

WORLD CHALLENGE is intended exclusively for individuals aged 18 and older. You must be at least 18 years old to create or use an account. We do not knowingly permit individuals under 18 to use the application or collect their personal information. If we become aware that an account belongs to someone under 18, we may suspend or delete that account.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect and process the following information:

  • Account information: email address, authentication identifier, and password credentials handled securely by our authentication provider.
  • Social login information: email address and account identifier received through Google or Facebook sign-in.
  • Profile information: username and selected country code.
  • Game information: points, spin credits, daily streaks, puzzle activity, and related game progress.
  • Referral information: referral codes and referral-related activity.
  • Technical information: authentication session information and data stored locally on your device.

The application does not currently request access to your contacts, photos, or precise device location.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to create and manage accounts, authenticate users, operate game features, maintain leaderboards, operate referral features, prevent abuse, maintain application security, troubleshoot technical problems, and support account deletion requests.

4. Public Leaderboards

Your username, selected country code, and game points may be displayed in public player rankings within the application. Please do not use personal or sensitive information as your username.

5. Third-Party Services

WORLD CHALLENGE uses third-party services that may process information as necessary to provide the application:

  • Supabase: authentication, database services, and application backend.
  • Google Mobile Ads: advertising services and related technical processing.
  • Google Sign-In: account authentication.
  • Facebook Login: account authentication.
  • Expo: application development, build, and distribution services.

These providers process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

6. Advertising

The application includes Google Mobile Ads SDK integration. Advertising services may process device identifiers, IP address, and other technical information as described in Google's privacy documentation. Advertising identifiers and test advertising configurations may be used during development and testing.

7. Data Storage and Security

Account and game information is processed through Supabase services. Authentication credentials are handled by the authentication provider. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Account Deletion

Users can request account deletion through the Profile section of the application using the Delete Account option. We will process deletion requests and remove or de-identify associated personal information where technically possible and legally required. Certain records may need to be retained where required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or legitimate operational purposes.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the application, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the reason it is processed.

10. International Data Transfers

Our service providers may process or store information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, appropriate safeguards will be applied to international transfers.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information; object to or restrict certain processing; and withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. You may contact us using the information below to exercise applicable rights.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application features, services, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published at the application's official Privacy Policy URL.
